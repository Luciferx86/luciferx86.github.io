Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 4, 2026

1. Introduction

Miscridex (“we”, “our”, “the app”) is a helper app for the online game Miscrits. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. By using Miscridex, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect limited information when you choose to create a Miscridex account:

  • Email address
  • Gamer tag / username

If you choose to verify your Miscrits game account through Miscridex, we may also collect:

  • Your in-game username (IGN)
  • Your in-game user ID, which is used internally for account verification and battle data synchronization

For verified users whose battles are synchronized with Miscridex, we may collect game-related battle data, including:

  • Both players' in-game usernames
  • Both players' in-game user IDs
  • Team compositions used in the battle
  • Ranking points earned or lost during the battle
  • Battle statistics and analytics generated from this data

Your in-game user ID is used internally for verification, account linking, and battle data processing. It is not displayed to other users.

Additionally, Miscridex retrieves game-related data and images from a public source (worldofmiscrits.com) to display Miscrit information. No personal user data is sent to or collected by this source.

We also collect anonymized diagnostic, performance, and usage information through Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and Firebase Performance Monitoring (services provided by Google LLC) to help us understand app performance and improve stability. This may include events such as app installs, updates, screen views, and in-app interactions.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Allow you to log in and save favorite Miscrits
  • Personalize your app experience
  • Verify ownership of Miscrits game accounts
  • Link verified Miscrits accounts with Miscridex accounts
  • Synchronize battle information associated with verified accounts
  • Generate battle analytics and player statistics
  • Enable verified users to search for and view other verified players
  • Display battle history, analytics, and featured player information to other verified users
  • Understand general app usage and improve performance
  • Identify and fix technical issues and crashes

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your personal information with third parties except where required by law or where necessary for the services described in this Privacy Policy.

Verified Miscridex users are automatically searchable by their in-game username. Certain game-related profile information is available to other authenticated and verified Miscridex users, including:

  • In-game username (IGN)
  • Battle analytics and performance statistics
  • Battle history and battle logs
  • Team compositions and ranking point changes shown in battle logs
  • Featured player achievements or titles

This information is not available to logged-out or unverified users. Your email address and in-game user ID are not displayed to other users.

Anonymized analytics and diagnostic data collected by Firebase are processed in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Third-Party Data Sources

Our app displays information and images provided by worldofmiscrits.com. While we do not share your personal data with them, their own privacy policy and terms may apply to the content they provide. Miscridex is not affiliated with or endorsed by the creators of worldofmiscrits.com or the Miscrits game.

6. Analytics and Diagnostic Tools

Miscridex uses Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and Firebase Performance Monitoring (all provided by Google LLC) to collect anonymized data about app usage, crashes, and performance metrics. This information helps us understand how users interact with the app, improve stability, and enhance user experience.

These services may automatically collect information such as device type, operating system, app version, screen views, feature usage, and crash logs. This data does not include personally identifiable information such as your name, email, or precise location, and it is not used for advertising or tracking across apps.

You can learn more about how Google handles Firebase data by visiting the Firebase Privacy and Security page.

If you prefer not to share analytics data, you can opt out through your device’s privacy settings (for example, “Opt out of Ads Personalization” or “Limit Ad Tracking”).

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal data. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

8. Verified Player Profiles

Verified Miscrits players are discoverable by other authenticated and verified Miscridex users through the player search feature.

A verified player's profile may include their in-game username, battle analytics, battle history and battle logs, team information, ranking point changes, and featured player achievements or titles.

Miscridex does not display a verified player's email address or in-game user ID to other users.

9. Children's Privacy

Miscridex is intended for users of all ages. If you are under 13 (or under 16 depending on your local regulations), please get parental consent before providing personal information.

10. Your Rights

You may:

  • Request deletion of your Miscridex account and associated personal information
  • Request removal of your verified player profile
  • Request deletion of your battle logs, battle analytics, and verification data
  • Contact us with questions about your data

When your Miscridex account is deleted, all data associated with your account, including verification information, battle data, analytics, and player profile information, is deleted from our systems.
